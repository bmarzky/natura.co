# Natura House Business Rules V1

## Product

Classic Black Forest
- Size: 20x20 cm
- Price: Rp250.000

## Production

Maximum production:
- 3 cakes per day

Minimum preorder:
- H-1

## Payment

Payment status:
- pending
- paid

Order is considered confirmed only after payment is verified.

## Delivery

Delivery area:
- sekitar Kota Juang, Bireuen

Delivery fee:
- determined separately

Delivery time:
- agreed with customer

## Order Changes

Customer may request changes to:
- quantity
- delivery date
- delivery address
- cake writing

Changes must be checked against the current order status.

## Cancellation

Cancellation requests must be checked against the current order status.

## AI Safety

AI must not:
- invent product information
- invent prices
- invent delivery fees
- promise availability without checking
- confirm payment without verification
- change order status without authorization
- promise delivery times without confirmation

If information is unavailable or uncertain:
- ask the customer
- or hand off to a human

## Human Handoff

Human assistance is required for:
- complaints
- refund requests
- payment problems
- special requests
- uncertain situations
- low-confidence intent
